Leadership & Practice Areas

How leadership, mentoring, documentation, coordination, and technical/operational literacy translate to business analysis, project support, and leadership-track roles — strictly tied to my CV.

A note on accuracy: This page describes practice areas drawn directly from my CV experience (tutoring, mentoring, ambassador work, and operational roles). I do not claim separate client projects, heavy software engineering work, or quantified outcomes here — because my CV does not evidence them.

1 · Leadership & Mentoring Practice

Relevant for: Mentoring · Team Leadership · Onboarding

Structured support for learners and new students — from 1-on-1 tutoring to university-wide orientation and community integration.

  • Online Academic Tutor & Mentor: 1-on-1 instruction with digital whiteboard and tablet tools, tailored to learning styles.
  • International Student Ambassador & Super Mentor (Sheffield Hallam University): orientation events, campus tours, structured academic mentoring, community integration.
  • Hallam Gold, Silver & Bronze Leadership Awards: recognition of leadership and mentoring contributions.

2 · Stakeholder Communication & Documentation

Relevant for: Business Analysis · Stakeholder Engagement

Translating needs and information into clear, usable communication — for students, staff, and operational teams.

  • Client communication: clear, audience-aware interaction across university and front-of-house settings.
  • Technical documentation: writing that helps others understand, decide, and act.
  • Tools for clarity: Excalidraw for visualizing ideas, Notion for organizing knowledge, MS Office / Google Workspace for collaborative documentation.

3 · Project & Workflow Coordination

Relevant for: Project Support · Operations · Coordination

Keeping work organized, on time, and coordinated across people and tasks — in study and in fast-paced operations.

  • Project coordination & workflow optimization: organizing tasks, time, and handoffs; improving how work flows.
  • Agile Methodologies: working in Agile ways — iterative, collaborative, and responsive to feedback.
  • Time management under pressure: reliable execution when pace and priorities shift.

4 · Operational Execution

Relevant for: Operations · Front-of-House · Service Roles

Disciplined, team-based delivery in fulfillment, hospitality, and care environments.

  • Amazon Fulfillment Center, Nando’s, Care Assistant: operational and front-of-house experience.
  • Cross-functional teamwork: coordinating with colleagues across roles to get work done.
  • Safety compliance & task execution under pressure: maintaining standards while delivering to time.

5 · Technical Literacy for Analysis

Relevant for: Business Analysis · Tech-Adjacent Roles

A shared language with technical teams — enough depth to analyze, document, and coordinate credibly, without misrepresenting myself as a heavy coding candidate.

  • Database Fundamentals [SQL] & Version Control [Git]: understanding data and collaborative change.
  • Key modules: Databases and Web Fundamentals; Software Architecture & Design; Secure Software Engineering; Algorithms and Data Structures.
  • Analytical problem-solving: breaking down problems, structuring information, and supporting decisions.

Professional summary

I’m Adithyan Santhakumar (Adhi), a BSc (Hons) Computer Science with Artificial Intelligence (2:1) graduate from Sheffield Hallam University, graduated June 2026 and based in Sheffield, UK.

My strengths are in leadership, student mentoring, 1-on-1 tutoring, clear communication, project coordination, workflow optimization, technical documentation, and operational execution under pressure. As an International Student Ambassador & Super Mentor and an Online Academic Tutor & Mentor, I have supported learners through structured mentoring, orientation and community integration, and individual instruction tailored to learning styles. In operational and front-of-house roles at Amazon Fulfillment Center, Nando’s, and as a Care Assistant, I have worked to high standards of time management, cross-functional teamwork, and safety compliance.

I am positioning for tech-adjacent, business analysis, project support, and operational leadership-track roles — where the ability to communicate clearly, coordinate work, document decisions, and understand technical constraints adds value. My degree provides the technical literacy to do that credibly; it is not a claim to heavy software engineering roles.
